# Daily Deep Dive: 2026-10-07

## Authority Must Survive Execution

The new Agent Trust Runtime research connects a human principal, delegated agent,
mandate, proposed action, deterministic controls, risk and execution evidence.
A signed permission is useful only if the executor actually verifies it and
cannot bypass its constraints through the supported integration.

Before exposing a usable credential, record durable authority and reserve the
shared budget/goal. A lost external response creates an unknown outcome, not a
fresh permission to spend. Retain exposure until reconciliation; make revocation
orderings and the execution cutoff explicit. Redis TTL and signature validity
alone cannot prove concurrency-safe economic state.

## Semantic Similarity Is Not Consent

A purchase can sound appropriate while violating a hard condition. The supplied
example requires approval above INR 140,000 yet shows ALLOW at INR 146,999 without
that evidence. Correct flow: challenge, action-bound approval, re-evaluation,
then a narrowly bound capability. A high model score cannot bypass this obligation.
The user-visible approval must bind the exact action; changed actions need the
appropriate new authority.

## Expiry Does Not Resolve An External Side Effect

Version 1.1 correctly treats a payment timeout as unknown, but its risk table
still allows policy-defined expiry of the reserved budget. If a rail accepted
the request before the response was lost, expiry cannot make that payment cease
to exist. Release unused capacity only after dispatch is durably fenced and no
existing attempt can have taken effect. After possible dispatch, retain exposure
through authoritative reconciliation or an explicit accounted resolution that
does not silently replenish spending authority.

The demo also illustrates why scenarios require lifecycle truth. Reserving
INR 136,999 under a one-laptop INR 150,000 mandate leaves INR 13,001 and an occupied
quantity slot. Approval of a later INR 146,999 proposal does not replace those
facts. Use distinct fixtures/mandates or prove safe cancellation first.

The source drawing separates AUTHORIZATION_COMMITTED and BUDGET_RESERVED. An
intermediate state can exist only if it cannot grant usable authority; the
implementation needs explicit transactions, crash recovery and legal transition
tests. A corrected narrative is design progress, not executable invariant proof.

## Planning Clarity And Proof Are Different Deliverables

A clear handoff chooses among conflicting source recommendations while keeping
the original sources and traceability. The plan retains Neo4j/OPA and merchant
isolation, maps agent step-up/evidence requests to typed CHALLENGE obligations,
and gives each re-evaluation a new immutable decision. REPLAN denies the exact
proposed action and invites a new proposal; quarantine is containment state.

Final reservation/authorization must recheck authority after slow enrichment.
Its PostgreSQL commit precedes usable credentials, and a separate fenced dispatch
commit defines revocation's cutoff. Unknown side effects retain exposure. These
are selected design requirements; concrete schema/lock/protocol tests still have
to prove them when the implementation begins.

The UX must communicate these distinctions: allowed is not paid, expired is not
failed, and simulated is not observed. The twin experiment needs personal fidelity
proof; customer adoption needs commercial evidence. Product clarity can resolve
engineering choices without fabricating either result.

## Version 1.2: A Correct Rule Still Needs An Enforcement Boundary

The new research states the right exposure rule: permission may expire while an
uncertain payment continues to hold budget. A failure sequence explains why a
local status flag is insufficient. A worker sends a payment, the provider accepts
it, and the worker crashes before recording submission. A cleanup worker now sees
an old reservation with no recorded success. Releasing it could permit a second
purchase while the first is real. The durable dispatch claim must precede the
external call, survive restart and retain exposure until reconciliation proves
an outcome. A stale worker must not send after safe cancellation has won.

Similarly, explicit action-hash binding is a strong design improvement, but the
contract still must define the bytes being hashed and prove that amount,
destination, currency or mandate changes invalidate approval. A simulation of a
person's likely purchase cannot supply this missing authority. The review records
these implementation obligations without claiming their proof already exists.

why are token expiry and a missing local success record insufficient?
