## 2. Payment Processing Concepts Learned

- A domain event reports committed payment truth; it must never be replayed as
  permission to call a processor or mutate a ledger.
- Source, event, and pending-publication rows share one database transaction;
  Kafka is intentionally after commit and therefore at least once.

## 3. Fraud Detection Concepts Learned

- Useful analytics require decision-time feature/rule versions, later label
  availability, traffic origin, freshness, and coverage—not merely a score.
- PARTIAL coverage is a product fact: counts may be useful, but incomplete money
  totals must be withheld.

## 4. Security Concepts Learned

- Merchant risk roles and platform data operators are separate identities.
- Tenant-qualified keys are checked across Kafka key, event bytes, inbox,
  generation, projection, API, rebuild, and dataset boundaries.
- Payloads, credentials, notes, URLs, identifiers, digests, and raw exception
  text are excluded from logs/metric dimensions and recovery responses.

## 5. Architecture / Design Notes

- PostgreSQL owns atomic truth, deduplication, leases, fencing, append-only
  evidence, and active-generation swaps. Spring Kafka owns broker mechanics;
  Spring Batch owns durable chunk/restart mechanics.
- Rebuild bootstraps pre-event rows with immutable source facts rather than
  inventing historical events. V81 caps each catch-up request at 100 inputs;
  V82 preserves the canonical `0..10000` basis-point score domain.
- Timestamps order evidence but do not identify it. V83–V85 make the exact
  source relationship relational, tenant-qualified, immutable, event-type
  checked, and bound to the decision's real payment stream.

## 7. Bugs, Surprises, Or Tradeoffs

- PostgreSQL JDBC does not map `timestamptz` directly to `Instant` here;
  `JdbcTime` now converts results through `Timestamp`, and analytics binds all
  range/cursor instants explicitly. Live proof registered and resolved
  `ER-0074`.
- The producer's `riskScore=500` meant 500 basis points, while V70 allowed only
  `0..100`; live projection/rebuild proof registered `ER-0075`, added V82,
  proved 500/10001 partitions on H2/PostgreSQL, and made the demo require stream
  version 5 rather than accepting a partially consumed payment.
- The advertised demo had drifted from `datasetId`/`SUCCEEDED`, publication-
  attempt and inbox schema names, and asserted only two of five durable counts.
  `ER-0076` corrected the client and made every durable hop mandatory.
- The first catch-up implementation paged by 100 but looped without a request
  bound. The hostile pass added durable 100-item cycles and monotonic evidence.
- Bootstrap reconciliation initially checked lineage/count/nonnegative values
  but could miss a positive amount change. It now independently rebuilds every
  no-post-cutoff merchant bucket from immutable facts and rejects corruption.
- PostgreSQL may choose either of two valid covering analytics indexes; proof
  now accepts both while still rejecting a sequential scan.
- `ER-0077` closed the machine-to-human unit gap: basis points remain the API
  contract and render as `87.00%`, not `8700`.
- `ER-0078` removed timestamp equality from replay identity and rejected
  same-time and cross-payment source substitution through V83–V85.
- `ER-0079` closed integer narrowing, timestamp precision/parse, and UUID
  representation partitions before projection handling.
- `ER-0080` made summary, time series, and generation history as losslessly
  continuable as payment search and removed the UI's 12-point slice.
- `ER-0081` made hour/day ranges UTC-aligned half-open intervals with distinct
  explicit-future rejection and server-owned default boundaries.
- `ER-0082` made the architecture source mandatory in the case-insensitive
  phase-closure gate and reconciled its ownership claims.
